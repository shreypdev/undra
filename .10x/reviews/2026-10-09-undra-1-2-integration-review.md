## Verdict

**Clean after the fixes below; nothing above Low stays open on this branch.** The release holds together: `undra drift` on
the fixtures prints what the goldens, the help, the testing page and the landing page show; a fresh `undra init` project
tests, regenerates and checks on the 1.2 CLI; the committed Bazel bindings are what the core generates; the root Swift
package builds; every version file says 1.2.0 and the migration notes are the two records'; the CI restructure drops no step
and every roll-up needs every job. What I found: the release's headline proof for ADR-066 (the compile test under default
main-actor isolation) was not running in the Gate, because the macOS image's default Xcode is 16.4 (Swift 6.1) and the test
skips below 6.2 (F1); the post's Swift registration line called a method the runtime does not have (F2); the architect
record claimed ignores the code does not have (F3); the design's sentence about not writing down where the requirement came
from contradicted the ADR, the CTO record and the post (F4). Four Lows are recorded and left.
